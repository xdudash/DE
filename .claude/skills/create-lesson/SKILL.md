# Create or revise a lesson

1. Read the knowledge model, content standard, source register, approved schema, and gold-standard lesson.
2. Confirm target learner, CEFR range, goal, concept IDs, prerequisites, explanation language(s), objectives, and exercise types.
3. If the required schema or review workflow is missing, do not invent a parallel contract; propose a task to define it first.
4. Draft natural German with context, clear explanation, examples, relevant boundaries, and justified exercise distractors.
5. Include accepted answer variants and explanatory feedback where appropriate.
6. Record provenance and rights metadata for external material; do not copy protected content without permission.
7. Run schema/reference validation on changed items.
8. Perform a separate linguistic and pedagogical review; structural validation is not a substitute.
9. Leave content unapproved until required reviews are recorded.
10. Report content IDs, files, checks, source/licensing state, and unresolved review points.

Do not generate large batches until a gold-standard lesson is accepted.
