# A1 Imperatives Lesson Design

## Goal
Make the optional imperative station on `route.core.a1_sentence_foundations` startable from Home after the learner completes the yes/no question lesson.

## Learner outcome
Recognize and form common short instructions addressed to one person (`du`), a group (`ihr`), and a person formally (`Sie`). Understand that separable prefixes stay at the end and that `bitte` softens a request.

## Scope
- One A1 unit, `unit.syntax.imperative.a1`, with 9 exercises across 8 supported formats.
- Use common forms (`Mach!`, `Macht!`, `Machen Sie!`, `Warten Sie!`) and one separable verb (`aufmachen`).
- Explain that some verbs have special forms; defer a full irregular-verb treatment and pragmatic command/request distinctions.
- Localize the unit to English, Russian and Ukrainian. Keep review metadata pending; localizations are drafts.
- Add an end-to-end route journey from completed prior units to the imperative lesson.

## Non-goals
No backend changes, automated speech assessment, human review claims, or route reordering. The station remains optional in the route definition.

## Acceptance
1. Content validation reports no errors or missing localization keys.
2. A seeded learner who completed the preceding route units sees and can open the imperative lesson as the next recommendation.
3. The lesson remains clearly marked internally as awaiting linguistic, pedagogical and rights review.
