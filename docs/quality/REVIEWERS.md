# Reviewers

Status: **all roles unassigned.** The project cannot approve content until people are assigned to these roles. The agent does not name people it has not been given.

| Role | Checks | Assigned to | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| German linguist (native speaker or qualified teacher) | Grammar, examples, audio check | — | unassigned |
| Pedagogy reviewer | Level fit, lesson load, exercises | — | unassigned |
| Ukrainian translation reviewer | Explanations in uk | — | unassigned |
| Russian translation reviewer | Explanations in ru | — | unassigned |
| Rights reviewer | Sources, licences, owner confirmation | — | unassigned |

## Where to find people
- German teachers at adult-education centres (Volkshochschule, Goethe-Institut, Ukrainian or Russian-speaking teachers of German) who take freelance review work.
- Native speakers of German in university language departments.
- Ukrainian and Russian speakers with C1 or higher German, for the translation roles.

## Request text (to send to a candidate)
> Hello! I'm building DeutschGo, a German course for life, work and study in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. I'm looking for a reviewer for lesson content: checking grammar explanations and exercises for correctness and level (A1). It is paid review work by the lesson. Would you be interested? I can send a sample lesson.

## Rule
A role is marked **assigned** only after a named person has agreed. Each sign-off goes into the `review` block of the entity it approves.
