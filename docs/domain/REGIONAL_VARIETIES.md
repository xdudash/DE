# Regional Varieties (DACH)

Status: **Accepted** (2026-10-12).

## Model
- Canonical content targets **standard German** (`variety: standard`), the shared written standard of DE/AT/CH.
- National standard variants are labelled: `de-DE`, `de-AT`, `de-CH`. Broader regional usage: `south`, `north`. Register labels: `neutral, formal, informal, colloquial, written, dated`.
- The learner's region preference changes **which examples and notes are shown first**, never the canonical facts.

## What to label
- Lexical differences (Jänner/Januar, Velo/Fahrrad, Paradeiser/Tomate, Matura/Abitur), administrative terms (Meldezettel/Anmeldebescheinigung, AMS/Agentur für Arbeit, RAV), pragmatic conventions (Grüezi, Servus), Swiss orthography (no ß), and notable pronunciation differences.
- Grammar differences only where real and relevant to learners (e.g. perfect auxiliary *sein* with *liegen/sitzen/stehen* in the south and Austria/Switzerland).
- Do not overstate differences: if a rule is identical (e.g. V2), say so (see the gold unit's regional note).

## Exercises
Accepted answers must include valid national variants where the exercise does not specify a region (e.g. Swiss `ss` spellings). If an exercise targets one variety, the prompt states it.

## Administrative and life information
Procedures differ by country and change over time. Scenario content about procedures carries a country label, a "last verified" date, and links to official sources; it is reviewed on a schedule (P5 task) rather than presented as timeless fact.
